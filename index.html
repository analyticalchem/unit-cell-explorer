Drag to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Keyboard: focus the view, then arrow keys rotate, + and − zoom, Home resets.

Spheres are hard spheres sized so nearest neighbors touch. Atoms shared between cells are always cut at the faces between them.

Color by site: corner atoms sit on the cell corners, body-center atoms in the middle of the cell, face-center atoms in the middle of each face.

HCP interior atoms sit inside the cell.

Layers (FCC): close-packed layers stack A, B, C, A… along the body diagonal.

In HCP they stack A, B, A… up the c axis.

HCP atoms start at 57% of touching size so the interior atom is not cut.

Split by layer (FCC blocks): the explode slider pulls the close-packed layers apart instead of the cells.

This view needs WebGL 2, which this browser has turned off or does not support.

Arrow keys rotate the crystal, plus and minus zoom, Home resets the view.
